使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Keep all chrome monochrome — black, white, gray only。
  • 建议：Reserve the Instagram gradient for Stories rings, the Create action, and brand moments。
  • 建议：Use SF Pro — never introduce a custom body typeface。
  • 建议：Bleed media edge-to-edge with zero corner radius on photos and videos。
  • 建议：Use outlined icons by default, filled icons for active/selected state。

避免

  • 避免：introduce new accent colors beyond the defined palette — the monochrome chrome is the brand。
  • 避免：apply the brand gradient to buttons, text, or general UI — it's reserved。
  • 避免：round feed photo corners — edge-to-edge squares and portraits are sacred。
  • 避免：add text labels to the tab bar — the icons do the work。
  • 避免：use large-title nav bars — Instagram is compact-title throughout。
