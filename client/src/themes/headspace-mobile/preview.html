使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Butter Cream {#FFF7E7} for the canvas — never pure white, never cool gray。
  • 建议：Use Marigold {#FF6B35} as the primary action color — Start, Continue, Save。
  • 建议：Apply the Aurora gradient (Marigold → Butter → Coral) on Today hero and meditation full-bleed cards。
  • 建议：Render the breathing sphere as a radial gradient ({#FFB89E} highlight → {#FF6B35} mid → {#E55A2B} shadow) with continuous breath animation。
  • 建议：Use Apercu (or Nunito as fallback) — soft humanist sans, never grotesque。

避免

  • 避免：use pure white as the canvas — Headspace is warm-cream by brand。
  • 避免：use harsh black {#000000} for text — Ink Brown {#2E1A47} is the value。
  • 避免：use UPPERCASE on Apercu — title case only, never all-caps。
  • 避免：replace illustration companions with photography — Headspace is fully illustrated。
  • 避免：use cool blues or grotesque sans-serif typography — the brand is warm and humanist。
