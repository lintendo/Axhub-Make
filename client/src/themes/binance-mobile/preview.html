使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the deep canvas {#0B0E11} as the default — Binance is dark-native。
  • 建议：Make Binance Yellow {#F0B90B} the *only* brand accent (CTA, active tab, links)。
  • 建议：Set ALL numbers in monospace with tabular figures so columns align。
  • 建议：Keep green {#0ECB81} = up/buy and red {#F6465D} = sell/down — absolute, never inverted。
  • 建议：Use dense 12pt row padding and 1pt {#2B3139} hairline dividers in lists。

避免

  • 避免：use white or any color other than {#F0B90B} for the primary CTA。
  • 避免：render prices/percentages in a proportional sans — alignment breaks。
  • 避免：invert up/down colors in light mode (green stays up, red stays down)。
  • 避免：pad rows generously "to feel friendly" — density is the Binance identity。
  • 避免：put white text on the yellow button (fails contrast and is off-brand)。
