使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Snow white ({#FFFFFF}) as the primary canvas — not off-white, not beige。
  • 建议：Reserve Feather Green ({#58CC02}) for the primary action and correct state — it's the verb。
  • 建议：Build buttons with the 3D ledge (4pt darker-tone offset below) — the chunky feel is the brand。
  • 建议：Pair every correct/wrong answer with both avisible feedback state and a sound — the game feel needs both channels。
  • 建议：Let Duo the owl carry emotional moments — empty states, celebrations, streak saves, loss screens。

避免

  • 避免：use sophisticated or muted palettes — Duolingo is deliberately bright and playful。
  • 避免：use shadows as your depth model — use solid-color 4pt ledges instead。
  • 避免：use pure black text ({#000000}) for body — use Eel ({#4B4B4B}) for softer warmth。
  • 避免：add more accent colors — the 6-role palette (green/red/orange/yellow/blue/purple) is complete。
  • 避免：use thin or light font weights — Duolingo type starts at weight 400 and lives at 700-800。
