使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Waze Purple {#7E55BE} as the brand primary — logo, polyline, FAB, "Go" button。
  • 建议：Use Waze Cyan {#33CCFF} as the secondary — current-location puck, info glyphs。
  • 建议：Render hazard reports as chunky cartoon speech bubbles with rounded 14pt corners, full-saturation colors, white icons inside, and a triangular tail pointing to the hazard location。
  • 建议：Use Boing typography (or SF Pro Rounded as fallback) — roundness is the brand。
  • 建议：Use Boing Mono on every numeric — distance, time, speed, ETA。

避免

  • 避免：use a plain blue location dot — Waze's puck is a CYAN ARROW, not a circular dot。
  • 避免：render hazard reports as small web-accessible icon pins — they're chunky cartoon speech bubbles with tails。
  • 避免：tone down the cartography — bright cyan water and mint parks are the signature; making them subtle defeats the brand。
  • 避免：replace Boing with a sharp grotesque (Helvetica, Inter without rounding) — the warmth comes from roundness。
  • 避免：make the "Go" button anything other than full-pill 56pt-tall Waze Purple。
