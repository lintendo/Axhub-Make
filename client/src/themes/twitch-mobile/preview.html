使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#0E0E10} near-black canvas — faint cool tint, quiet chrome so content/chat carry energy。
  • 建议：Keep the color split strict: Twitch Purple ({#9146FF}) = brand/action; Live Red ({#EB0400}) = liveness only。
  • 建议：Use off-white {#EFEFF1} for primary text — softer than pure white for long chat reading。
  • 建议：Put the red "● LIVE" pill top-left and the black viewer-count pill bottom-left on live thumbnails。
  • 建议：Encode presence in the avatar ring — purple normally, red when currently LIVE。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} for the app canvas — that's the theater/player surface only。
  • 避免：use Live Red for anything except liveness — it must always mean "happening now"。
  • 避免：use Twitch Purple for the LIVE state — purple is action/brand, never status。
  • 避免：use pure white {#FFFFFF} for body text — {#EFEFF1} is the intentional softer choice。
  • 避免：use thin or medium weights — Roobert here is 400 / 600 / 700 only。
