使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use PayPal Blue {#003087} for primary CTAs ("Send", "Continue") and active tab states — it's the core brand action color。
  • 建议：Use PayPal Sky {#0070BA} as the secondary accent — link color, icon color, gradient highlight。
  • 建议：Render the wordmark with the dual-blue P-P overlap — sky behind, blue front, NEVER a single color。
  • 建议：Use PayPal Sans Big for headings, balance amounts, button text — the rounded display weight is the brand voice。
  • 建议：Use PayPal Sans Small for body, metadata, transaction subtitles — Big at 14pt is wrong。

避免

  • 避免：render the wordmark in a single solid color — the dual-blue P-P overlap is the brand。
  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} for primary text — {#001435} (slight blue tint) is correct。
  • 避免：use vivid greens or yellows as primary accents — green and amber are reserved for status (success / pending)。
  • 避免：replace PayPal Sans with Helvetica or generic sans — the rounded terminals are intentional and warm。
  • 避免：use sharp-corner buttons — primary CTAs are soft pills (radius equals half-height)。
