使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the committed dark theater ({#1A1A1A}) inside a call so video is the brightest element。
  • 建议：Reserve Zoom Blue ({#2D8CFF}) for the primary action (Join/Start), active controls, and links。
  • 建议：Reserve in-call red ({#E02828}) for Leave/End and the muted-mic state only。
  • 建议：Make the Join button oversized (48pt tall, 17pt bold) — it is the app's center of gravity。
  • 建议：Use rounded 8pt video tiles with small corner name labels so faces dominate。

避免

  • 避免：keep a light background inside a call — it competes with participant video。
  • 避免：introduce a third accent color — blue for action, red for leave/mute, gray for everything else。
  • 避免：make name labels large — 13pt max; the gallery is about faces, not text。
  • 避免：let the active-speaker border flicker — debounce it。
  • 避免：keep the control bar permanently visible over video — auto-hide is the behavior。
