使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use a warm charcoal canvas ({#1A1A1A}) — a paper-at-night reading room, not cold black。
  • 建议：Reserve Audible Orange ({#FF9900}) for the primary action, progress, active chapter, and CTA。
  • 建议：Pair Playfair Display (titles/headers) with Inter (body/UI) — and keep the roles strict。
  • 建议：Use the {gobackward.30} / {goforward.30} skip controls flanking a 72pt orange Play。
  • 建议：Wrap the player cover in an orange circular progress ring。

避免

  • 避免：use cold pure-black — Audible's warmth ({#1A1A1A}) is the literary signature。
  • 避免：set body or metadata in the serif — Playfair Display is headings/titles only。
  • 避免：add a second accent color — orange is the entire system。
  • 避免：use generic ±10s skip — the 30-second skip is Audible's signature transport。
  • 避免：use full-pill buttons — Audible buttons are 8pt rounded rects。
