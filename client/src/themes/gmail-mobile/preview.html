使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure white ({#FFFFFF}) as the light canvas — content is the color, not the chrome。
  • 建议：Position the Compose FAB bottom-right at 16pt from edges — never inline, never centered。
  • 建议：Use Gmail red ({#D93025}) ONLY for the Compose FAB and destructive actions — it's sacred。
  • 建议：Apply the Google 4-color palette to avatars, category chips, Smart Reply chips — never to structural UI。
  • 建议：Use Google Sans for titles/buttons, Roboto for body/metadata — don't mix within a block。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} on dark mode — use {#202124} (Google's standard)。
  • 避免：color the inbox chrome — the FAB is the only chromatic element; everything else is grayscale。
  • 避免：use circles for the Compose FAB — it's a 16pt squircle, not a pure circle。
  • 避免：show red anywhere outside of destructive actions and the FAB。
  • 避免：mix Google Sans and Roboto in the same text block — clear division。
