使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#000000} pure black as the canvas — not warm gray, not {#111}。
  • 建议：Use Cash Green {#00D632} as the ONLY accent — Pay button, success states, active tab glyph。
  • 建议：Set the $-amount in Cash Sans Mono at 96pt+ on the Pay screen — this is the signature。
  • 建议：Use Cash Sans Black (weight 900) for screen titles like "Money", "Card", "Activity"。
  • 建议：Render every $-amount and every $Cashtag in Cash Sans Mono with tabular numerals。

避免

  • 避免：use blue or navy anywhere — Cash App is anti-bank-app blue。
  • 避免：add shadows — Cash App is flat; depth is fill-color contrast only。
  • 避免：use {#111} or warm dark grays as canvas — pure {#000000} is core。
  • 避免：dim inactive tab icons — Cash App keeps them at full white opacity。
  • 避免：add tab bar labels — this is a hard rule。
