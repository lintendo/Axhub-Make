使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Keep the map at 60%+ of viewport on every primary screen — it's the product。
  • 建议：Use Google Blue ({#4285F4}) for the route polyline, location dot, and Directions FAB。
  • 建议：Float the top search bar as a pill with 16pt margins and a soft shadow。
  • 建议：Render the bottom sheet with 16pt top corners, visible grabber, three detents。
  • 建议：Use the four-color pin system: Red default, Green saved, Blue home/work, Orange category。

避免

  • 避免：color the map itself with brand colors — the map uses its own tile palette。
  • 避免：round map pins heavily — the tear-drop shape with 3pt white inner dot is the silhouette。
  • 避免：drop pin shadows — use a ground-hugging ellipse for on-world feel。
  • 避免：animate the map aggressively — camera moves are 0.3-0.6s ease, never snap。
  • 避免：stack more than 3 floating map controls trailing edge — it clutters the map。
