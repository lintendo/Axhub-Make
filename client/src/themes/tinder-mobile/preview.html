使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Center the swipe card in the viewport with 16pt margins and 32pt of breathing room below it。
  • 建议：Use the brand gradient {#FD267A → #FF6036} on Match screens and premium CTAs — never elsewhere。
  • 建议：Assign one color per action: Gold (Rewind), Red (Nope), Blue (Super Like), Green (Like), Purple (Boost)。
  • 建议：Render LIKE / NOPE / SUPER LIKE stamps rotated 15° with 4pt colored borders in italic display type。
  • 建议：Show the photo paginator at the top of the card as thin white segments。

避免

  • 避免：change the swipe card aspect ratio — 3:4 portrait is load-bearing to the gesture。
  • 避免：add text labels to the tab bar — icons only。
  • 避免：use the brand gradient on every button — reserved for Match screens and premium。
  • 避免：swap the action button colors — the 5-verb color system is the UI language。
  • 避免：use pure black ({#000000}) for body text — {#424242} is softer and on-brand。
