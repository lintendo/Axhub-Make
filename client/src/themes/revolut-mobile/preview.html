使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#0A0A0F} as the canvas — cool near-black, not pure {#000000}, not warm。
  • 建议：Reserve the violet→purple gradient ({#5B6BFF → #9C6BFF}) for the primary action, active tab, and the spent arc。
  • 建议：Use the solid {#6B5BFF} only where a gradient can't render (thin strokes, tiny icons, focus rings)。
  • 建议：Make the balance the largest element on the screen — 34–40pt weight 700, tabular。
  • 建议：Use tabular numerals for every balance, amount, and currency tile so columns align。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} or a warm dark — the canvas has a cool blue cast。
  • 避免：apply the gradient to text glyphs — it's a fill/stroke device only。
  • 避免：add a second accent hue — the gradient is the entire brand color story。
  • 避免：let a merchant name outweigh its amount, or an amount outweigh the hero balance。
  • 避免：use proportional figures for money — always tabular, decimal-aligned。
