使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#141414} as the canvas — warm dark charcoal, not pure black (except behind hero art)。
  • 建议：Reserve Netflix Red ({#E50914}) for the NETFLIX wordmark, Play button, and Continue Watching progress bar — nothing else。
  • 建议：Use the red wordmark as the ONLY header on Home — no text title, no search bar in the top app bar。
  • 建议：Render posters at a 2:3 aspect ratio with a 4pt corner radius — Netflix's signature tile。
  • 建议：Use the vertical gradient from canvas to transparent to fade hero images into the background。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} as the canvas — {#141414} is warmer and the Netflix standard。
  • 避免：ship a light mode — Netflix has always been dark-only。
  • 避免：use green for success (downloaded, checkmark states) — Netflix uses gray。
  • 避免：use a full-pill corner on the Play button — 4pt is signature。
  • 避免：use red anywhere other than the defined brand moments — no red borders, no red text, no red accent on metadata。
