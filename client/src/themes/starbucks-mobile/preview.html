使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Siren Green ({#00704A}) on every primary CTA — Add to Order, Pay, Order button。
  • 建议：Use House Green ({#1E3932}) as the dark canvas — very dark forest green, NOT black。
  • 建议：Place the Stars dashboard ring at the top of the Home tab — it's the daily habit hook。
  • 建议：Render the Stars count in SoDo Sans 48pt w800 gold {#CBA258} centered in the ring。
  • 建议：Use the 5-pill size selector for drinks (Short / Tall / Grande / Venti / Trenta)。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black for the dark canvas — {#1E3932} House Green is load-bearing。
  • 避免：use a banking-style blue for links — Info Blue is sparingly used。
  • 避免：use display type larger than 48pt — Stars count is the only display moment。
  • 避免：skip the size selector — it's the mental model of ordering at Starbucks。
  • 避免：collapse category cards — photography IS the content。
