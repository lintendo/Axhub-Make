使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#FF385C} as the primary action color for Reserve, save-heart fill, category underline, and active tab。
  • 建议：Use {#FF5A5F} (Rausch) for the Belo logomark and illustration moments — it's the heritage red that still appears。
  • 建议：Render stay-card photos at 4:3 with 16pt corner radius — this is the single most recognizable component in the app。
  • 建议：Place the save-heart top-right on every stay card, with a subtle drop shadow for legibility over varied photography。
  • 建议：Use Hof ({#484848}) for primary text, not {#000000} — Airbnb is warm, not hard。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} for body text — {#484848} (Hof) is the correct value。
  • 避免：render stay-card photos with sharp corners or rounded corners smaller than 16pt。
  • 避免：use Primary Coral for body text or section headers — it's a verb, not a color。
  • 避免：replace the Belo logomark's Rausch red with Primary Coral — they are intentionally different。
  • 避免：use aggressive shadows — stay cards are lifted by their radius, not their drop。
