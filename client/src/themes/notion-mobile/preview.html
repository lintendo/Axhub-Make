使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure white ({#FFFFFF}) as the light canvas — Notion is paper。
  • 建议：Use dark charcoal ({#191919}) for dark mode — NOT true black。
  • 建议：Use warm dark text ({#37352F}) — NOT pure black。
  • 建议：Surface block handles (⋮⋮⋮ + {+}) on long-press (mobile) or hover (desktop) — never always-visible。
  • 建议：Use the {/} command as the primary block-creation gesture — it's the heart of Notion。

避免

  • 避免：use card-style borders around content blocks — Notion is flat, content flows inline。
  • 避免：use saturated colors as page backgrounds — the 9 pastels are all near-greyscale。
  • 避免：hard-code Inter — users can switch to Lora (serif) or IBM Plex Mono。
  • 避免：surface block handles always — the "invisible" design is the signature。
  • 避免：use pure black ({#000000}) for body text — {#37352F} warm dark gray is correct。
