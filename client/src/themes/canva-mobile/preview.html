使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use a bright near-white workspace ({#FFFFFF} / {#F7F8FA}) light, warm-neutral ({#18191B}) dark — friendly, never harsh。
  • 建议：Reserve the cyan→purple gradient ({#00C4CC → #7D2AE8}) for brand + primary action (logo, create FAB, primary CTA, hero rails)。
  • 建议：Round everything generously — 12–16pt radii on cards/tiles/sheets, 999pt pills on buttons。
  • 建议：Build home as greeting + big rounded search + horizontal design-type tile rail + stacked template galleries。
  • 建议：Give each design-type tile its own gradient (Instagram, Presentation, Doc, Whiteboard, Video)。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black ({#000000}) for canvas or text — warm-neutral {#18191B} / near-black {#0D0E10}。
  • 避免：overuse the gradient — secondary buttons are solid purple or neutral outline, not gradient。
  • 避免：use sharp corners — Canva's softness (rounded, pill) is the entire brand feel。
  • 避免：dim or desaturate content thumbnails in dark mode — only the chrome dims。
  • 避免：put shadows on flat rows or chrome — shadows are for raised content cards and the FAB only。
