使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Google Blue {#1A73E8} for the FAB, primary CTAs, links, and the Today highlight。
  • 建议：Use the four Material event colors for system events: Blue meetings, Red declined, Yellow tasks, Green goals。
  • 建议：Honor the 24-color user-selectable calendar palette — let user-named calendars choose their tint。
  • 建议：Use Google Sans Display for nav titles and day numbers; SF Pro Text for body and rows on iOS。
  • 建议：Use TABULAR FIGURES on every time and date — "9:00 AM / 10:30 AM" must align。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} for text — {#202124} is Google's near-black。
  • 避免：bold-weight nav titles or section headers — Google Calendar uses Medium (500), almost never Bold。
  • 避免：apply event colors at full saturation in Day/Week tinted blocks — use 20% saturation for the fill, full saturation for the title text and 3pt left bar。
  • 避免：replace the Material dual-shadow with a single drop-shadow — the dual-shadow IS the Material look。
  • 避免：put the FAB anywhere other than bottom-right。
