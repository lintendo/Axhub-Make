使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the deep teal-black canvas {#001017}/{#002A3A} — cyan undertone, not neutral gray。
  • 建议：Make Fitbit Teal {#00B0B9} the brand/steps/CTA; brighten to {#21D9CE} for text/links on dark。
  • 建议：Give every metric its fixed hue and keep it everywhere (tile, ring, chart, detail) — color is a mnemonic。
  • 建议：Make the big number the visual hero of every card (40–44pt bold, tight tracking)。
  • 建议：Use progress rings as the recurring shape language for goals。

避免

  • 避免：use a neutral-gray dark theme — the canvas must carry the teal cast。
  • 避免：let metrics share or swap colors — sleep is always purple, HR always coral。
  • 避免：make labels/units compete with the hero number — keep them quieter and smaller。
  • 避免：use clinical phrasing ("78% goal completion") — Fitbit speaks like a kind coach。
  • 避免：use sharp or small radii — cards are 16–24pt, buttons are full pills。
