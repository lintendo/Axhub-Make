使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the deep space-navy canvas ({#0A0E2A}) with a faint starfield behind the hero。
  • 建议：Reserve Disney Blue ({#0063E5}) for primary actions; use glow blue ({#1A75FF}) for focus halos and selected tiles。
  • 建议：Make the brand-portal tile row (Disney/Pixar/Marvel/Star Wars/Nat Geo) a prominent, defining element。
  • 建议：Use 16:9 landscape cards — the streaming/TV aspect, never square music tiles。
  • 建议：Stage the hero as a full-bleed billboard with logo artwork and an auto-playing muted trailer。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black or a flat gray canvas — Disney+ is rich navy {#0A0E2A} with a starfield。
  • 避免：add a second brand hue — content artwork supplies the color。
  • 避免：use square (1:1) content tiles — Disney+ is 16:9 landscape。
  • 避免：replace logo artwork with plain text titles when branded logos exist。
  • 避免：drop the brand-portal row — it's the signature navigation。
