使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#121212} as the canvas — NOT true black, NOT {#191414}。
  • 建议：Reserve Spotify Green ({#1DB954}) for the primary "play/follow" action and active toggles — it's the verb。
  • 建议：Use black text ({#000000}) on green CTA buttons — not white, it's intentional for contrast。
  • 建议：Render album art with 6pt corner radius — softening without rounding。
  • 建议：Use Spotify Mix (or Circular as legacy fallback) at weights 400/600/700 only。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} as the canvas — it kills Now Playing gradient contrast。
  • 避免：add accent colors beyond green — the app's restraint is what makes the green pop。
  • 避免：use light weights — Spotify Mix starts at 400。
  • 避免：use large display-size headlines — content (album art, track titles) is the hero。
  • 避免：round album art heavily — 6pt max, keep the square integrity。
