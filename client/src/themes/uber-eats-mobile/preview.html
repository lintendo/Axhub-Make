使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use a pure white canvas ({#FFFFFF}) by default — photo-first, gallery-like whitespace (dark mode inverts neutrals only)。
  • 建议：Reserve Uber Eats Green ({#06C167}) for primary actions and selected states — cart bar, Add, Track, active pill。
  • 建议：Keep the same green ({#06C167}) in dark mode — only neutrals invert。
  • 建议：Let restaurant cards sit borderless and shadowless on white — the food photo provides the framing。
  • 建议：Keep ratings monochrome (near-black star) — quiet merchandising so green = action stands out。

避免

  • 避免：default to a dark canvas — the brand is bright and photo-first (dark mode is a supported alternate, not the default)。
  • 避免：add a second accent hue — the single green is the entire action language。
  • 避免：color the rating star yellow — Uber Eats keeps it monochrome and quiet。
  • 避免：put borders/shadows on restaurant cards — they sit flat on white; the photo frames itself。
  • 避免：use thin or black weights — Uber Move here is 400 / 500 / 700 only。
