使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use {#F3F2EF} cream as the canvas — NOT pure white, the desaturation is signature。
  • 建议：Reserve LinkedIn Blue ({#0A66C2}) for links, Connect/Follow, active states — it's the verb。
  • 建议：Use 90% black ({#000000E6}) for body text — softer than pure black。
  • 建议：Float white post cards on cream with 8pt gap between — no shadow, canvas separates them。
  • 建议：Underline hyperlinks (@mentions, #hashtags, inline URLs) at 1pt in LinkedIn Blue。

避免

  • 避免：use pure white {#FFFFFF} as the main canvas — it flattens the card structure。
  • 避免：add accent colors beyond LinkedIn Blue — the app's restraint is professional signaling。
  • 避免：use decorative illustrations — text + photos + media is the content language。
  • 避免：show a single generic "Like" — always offer the 6 reactions on long-press。
  • 避免：use display-size headlines — 24pt profile name is the upper bound。
