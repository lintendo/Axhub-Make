使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use pure black {#000000} as the default dark canvas — X is committed to true-black OLED。
  • 建议：Reserve Twitter Blue {#1D9BF0} for links, replies, verified marks, and the active tab underline。
  • 建议：Use role-specific colors on action icons: blue (reply/bookmark), green (repost), pink (like)。
  • 建议：Keep post rows flat with 1pt hairlines — no shadows, no cards, no elevation between posts。
  • 建议：Use the Chirp font at weights 400 and 700 only (with 500 for select labels) — no lights, no extra-bolds。

避免

  • 避免：use shadows to separate posts — hairlines are the brand-correct separator。
  • 避免：colorize post body text — body text is always primary neutral。
  • 避免：add more accent colors beyond blue / green / pink — they map 1:1 to reply / repost / like。
  • 避免：use uppercase on button labels — "Post", "Follow", "Subscribe" are in sentence case。
  • 避免：use thin font weights — Chirp starts at 400 and tops out at 700。
