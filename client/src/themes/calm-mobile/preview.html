使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use the night-sky gradient ({#2A6FD6} → {#0B1E3F}) as the canvas — dusk, not pure black。
  • 建议：Make cards glass — {rgba(255,255,255,0.08)} with backdrop blur so the sky shows through。
  • 建议：Reserve Calm Blue ({#2A6FD6}) for primary actions, the active tab, and progress。
  • 建议：Set headlines in Lora (serif) and body/UI in Inter (sans) — never swap the roles。
  • 建议：Give the layout generous air — 20pt margins, 1.55 body leading, 32pt sections。

避免

  • 避免：use pure black {#000000} as the canvas — Calm is a twilight gradient, not a void。
  • 避免：add saturated accent colors beyond Calm Blue (gold streak is the only rare warm)。
  • 避免：set headlines in a sans — the Lora serif is the brand's voice。
  • 避免：use hard drop shadows — depth is translucency and blur, not cast shadow。
  • 避免：crowd the layout — busyness is the opposite of calm。
