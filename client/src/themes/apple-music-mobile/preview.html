使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use SF Pro exclusively — Apple Music is Apple's flagship first-party app; resist adding custom fonts。
  • 建议：Use {#000000} as the dark canvas — full-contrast black, not {#121212}。
  • 建议：Use 12pt corner radius on album art tiles — larger than most media apps, Apple Music's signature。
  • 建议：Reserve Apple Music Red ({#FA2D48}) for the Play button, Now Playing indicator, heart, shuffle active, and scrubber fill。
  • 建议：Use iOS system materials ({translucent backdrop blur}) for nav, tab bar, and mini-player backgrounds。

避免

  • 避免：use a custom font — SF Pro covers every need in Apple Music。
  • 避免：use {#121212} or warmer dark canvases — Apple Music goes full {#000000}。
  • 避免：use 6pt corner radius on album art — 12pt is signature, softer。
  • 避免：use Apple Music Red for secondary actions, borders, or generic accents — use system blue, gray, or label colors。
  • 避免：roll your own Play button when web-accessible icon {play.fill} exists。
