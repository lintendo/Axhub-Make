使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Keep the map at 60%+ of viewport on every primary screen。
  • 建议：Use pure {#000000} for primary CTAs, destination pin, and active bottom-tab icon。
  • 建议：Render the "Where to?" bottom sheet with 16pt top-corner radius and visible grabber。
  • 建议：Use Uber Move Mono for every ETA, fare, and address — never the body text cut。
  • 建议：Reserve green {#05A357} for "driver arrived" and "confirmed" states only。

避免

  • 避免：add gradients, glows, or glossy effects — Uber's chrome is flat, period。
  • 避免：color primary CTAs — the primary button is always black, never branded green or blue。
  • 避免：use green or red outside their semantic roles (success / destructive) — they lose meaning。
  • 避免：round bottom sheet corners on all four sides — only top corners, bottom is flush。
  • 避免：use drop shadows heavier than 0.25 opacity anywhere — elevation stays whisper-quiet。
