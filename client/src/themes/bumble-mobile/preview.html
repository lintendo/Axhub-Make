使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Bumble Yellow ({#FFC629}) as the dominant action color — it's the brand。
  • 建议：Pair yellow with pure black ({#000000}) for text on yellow surfaces — WCAG AA requires it。
  • 建议：Use Brando weights 500/700/900 — the brand is heavy and confident。
  • 建议：Render the heart action button with a yellow glow shadow — {rgba(255, 198, 41, 0.5) 0 6px 16px}。
  • 建议：Use the hexagon shape for the brand mark, match notification photo crops, and mode toggle buttons。

避免

  • 避免：use white text on Bumble Yellow — contrast fails WCAG; always pure black。
  • 避免：use Bumble Yellow for body text — yellow is a verb, never type。
  • 避免：soften the photo aspect to 4:5 portrait (that's Hinge) — Bumble uses 3:4。
  • 避免：replace the hexagon with a circle on match notifications — the hex is the brand spatial signature。
  • 避免：use Brando Light or Thin — the brand doesn't have light weights。
