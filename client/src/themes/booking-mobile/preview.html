使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use clean white ({#FFFFFF}) as the canvas — the marketplace needs density on white。
  • 建议：Use Booking Blue ({#003580}) for brand/structure: app bar, Genius banner, the score badge。
  • 建议：Use CTA Blue ({#0071C2}) for action: Search, Reserve, links, selected filters。
  • 建议：Render the review-score badge as a solid-navy rounded chip with a one-decimal score + word。
  • 建议：Always format the score as {X.X} (one decimal) — {8.9}, never {8.90} or {8}。

避免

  • 避免：use a tinted or dark canvas — the dense card feed needs white。
  • 避免：blur the blue roles — navy is brand/structure, bright blue is action。
  • 避免：restyle the score badge per screen — its rigid navy + one-decimal format is the brand tell。
  • 避免：show the score as an integer or two decimals — always one decimal。
  • 避免：add accent colors beyond the two blues and the rating yellow。
