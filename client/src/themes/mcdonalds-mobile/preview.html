使用建议

建议

  • 建议：Use Golden Arches Yellow ({#FFC72C}) for the primary CTA, rewards number, active tab, and Order FAB。
  • 建议：Always put {#1A1A1A} near-black text/icons on yellow — it's a hard brand rule。
  • 建议：Use McDonald's Red ({#DA291C}) for deal flags, the secondary CTA, and urgency。
  • 建议：Make the MyMcDonald's Rewards card the home hero — big yellow points number + progress bar。
  • 建议：Lead every deal tile with appetizing product photography + a red flag。

避免

  • 避免：put white text on yellow — ever; near-black {#1A1A1A} only。
  • 避免：recolor or restyle the Golden Arches。
  • 避免：introduce a third loud brand color competing with yellow + red。
  • 避免：use sharp-cornered buttons — CTAs are pills, cards are 16pt rounded。
  • 避免：bury the rewards balance — it's the reason the app is opened; it leads the home screen。
